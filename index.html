About me

Thanh Châm

Senior Product · Branding Designer

1995 · Born in Ha Noi

Hi there! I’m a Designer with 5 years of experience in digital product design and 8 years in branding & graphic design. I enjoy crafting clean, intuitive experiences that connect users with products in a natural and meaningful way.

With a background in both user experience and visual identity, I focus on finding the sweet spot between usability and personality — making sure every design not only works but feels right. I love simple ideas, smooth user flows, and building things that actually help people.

Let’s connect and create something great together!
